What is Quizzard

A student-run team making quizzing a joy across Odisha

Founded in 2017 by six students from Cuttack, Quizzard brings quizzing to school students, teachers and parents through interactive, interesting formats.

Flagship event: Synapse

The largest quiz in Cuttack and one of the largest in Odisha, held every year since 2017.

Quiz workshops

Hands-on sessions in schools and colleges that teach students how to think, not just what to remember.

Student outreach

2500+ students reached in 2025 through workshops and interactive sessions across the state.

Professional quizzes

Researched, hosted and presented end to end for schools, organisations and government bodies.

What is Synapse

Curiosity, knowledge and fun at one junction

A homegrown quiz competition built on a love for quizzing and for the Millennium City. We don’t reward mugging up. Every question is made to be enjoyable and workable, so each one teaches something new about something familiar.

  • Questions that reward lateral thinking and common sense
  • Buzzer, audio-visual, grid and themed rounds
  • Audience-friendly, so everyone in the hall plays along

Synapse through the years

A decade of ideas, people and possibilities

2017

The first Synapse, in English: 79 participants from 20 schools.

2018

A bigger venue at Saheed Bhawan: 200 participants, 25 schools.

2019

Junior and Senior quizzes arrive: 750 participants, 50 schools.

2020

Our first online edition, live-streamed through the pandemic.

2021

Second online edition, and the first exclusive Odia Quiz.

2022

Back offline, with Odia Quiz prelims and finals on stage.

2023

Record 1100+ direct participants and 2000+ in 20 workshops.

2024

1200+ direct participants and 500+ in the Odia Quiz.

2025

1500+ direct participants and 2500+ outreach: our biggest yet.
